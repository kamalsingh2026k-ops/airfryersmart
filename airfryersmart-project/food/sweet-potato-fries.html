🍠 Air Fryer Sweet Potato Fries: Temperature, Time & Tips

Sweet potatoes have more sugar and moisture than russets, so they burn and sag more easily. Cornstarch and a lower temperature fix both problems.

🍠

Air fryer settings for Sweet Potato Fries

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 380°F (193°C) 16 min Shake at 8 min
From frozen 400°F (204°C) 15 min Shake once halfway
Conventional oven (for comparison) 425°F (218°C) 28 min —

Done by texture: crisp edges, tender centers. Sweet potato needs to cook through or it stays starchy.

How to make it

Prep: Soak cut fries in cold water 30 minutes to leach surface starch, then dry completely. This is the step everyone skips and it's the one that matters.

  1. Soak the cut fries in cold water for 30 minutes, then dry completely with towels.
  2. Toss with cornstarch until evenly dusted.
  3. Add oil and seasonings and toss again.
  4. Cook at 380°F for 8 minutes in a single layer.
  5. Shake and cook 8 more minutes until edges are crisp.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, cut into 1/4-inch fries
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne (optional)

Pro tips

  • ✅One tablespoon of cornstarch per pound creates a thin shell that turns genuinely crispy.
  • ✅380°F, not 400°F. Sweet potato sugars scorch before the inside softens at higher heat.
  • ✅Cut into uniform 1/4-inch batons. Thick-cut wedges will never crisp the same way.

Common questions

Why won't my sweet potato fries get crispy?

Excess moisture and no starch coating. Soak, dry thoroughly, and use cornstarch. Also cook in two batches rather than one crowded one.

Frozen sweet potato fries?

400°F for 15 minutes, shaking twice. Frozen ones are pre-treated and crisp more reliably than homemade.
