🧊 Air Fryer Crispy Tofu: Temperature, Time & Tips

The air fryer solves tofu. No shallow-frying, no sticking to the pan, and every single cube gets a golden crust.

🧊

Done when cubes are golden on multiple faces and firm-springy when pressed.

How to make it

Prep: Press extra-firm tofu for at least 20 minutes under something heavy. Then toss cubes with cornstarch — this is what creates the crust.

  1. Press tofu 20 minutes, then cut into 1-inch cubes.
  2. Toss cubes with soy sauce, then with oil.
  3. Dust with cornstarch and garlic powder, tossing until evenly coated.
  4. Cook at 390°F for 8 minutes in a single layer.
  5. Shake and cook 7 more minutes until golden; finish with sesame oil.

Ingredients

  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (finish)

Pro tips

  • ✅Extra-firm only. Silken or soft tofu disintegrates in the basket.
  • ✅One tablespoon of cornstarch per block is the difference between crispy and merely warm.
  • ✅Sauce it after cooking. Marinating wet tofu before air frying prevents any crust from forming.

Common questions

Do I have to press tofu?

For crispy results, yes. Water in the block turns to steam and prevents any surface browning. Twenty minutes under a heavy pan is enough.

Can I use frozen tofu?

Freeze and thaw it first — this creates a spongier, chewier texture that many people prefer. Press hard after thawing, then cook as usual.
