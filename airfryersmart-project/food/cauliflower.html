🥦 Air Fryer Cauliflower: Temperature, Time & Tips

Air frying turns cauliflower nutty and golden instead of the watery blandness you get from steaming.

🥦

Air fryer settings for Cauliflower

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 390°F (199°C) 14 min Shake at 7 min
From frozen 390°F (199°C) 18 min Shake once halfway
Conventional oven (for comparison) 425°F (218°C) 25 min —

Done when edges are deeply golden and a knife enters the stem easily.

How to make it

Prep: Cut florets so each has one flat cut face. Flat faces are where browning happens.

  1. Cut cauliflower into even florets with flat faces.
  2. Toss with oil and all seasonings.
  3. Spread in a single layer, flat sides down.
  4. Cook at 390°F for 7 minutes.
  5. Shake and cook 7 more minutes until browned at the edges.

Ingredients

  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1.5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Pro tips

  • ✅Cauliflower absorbs oil quickly — measure it rather than pouring, or it turns greasy.
  • ✅For buffalo cauliflower, sauce it in the last 3 minutes only.
  • ✅Turmeric or curry powder is especially good here; cauliflower is bland enough to take strong spice.

Common questions

Frozen cauliflower?

390°F for 18 minutes, shaking twice. Expect softer texture than fresh — there's no way around the ice content.

How do I make buffalo cauliflower?

Air fry plain for 11 minutes, toss in buffalo sauce, then return for 3 more minutes to set the glaze.
