🍔 Air Fryer Hamburgers: Temperature, Time & Tips

Ground beef needs to hit 160°F, which is exactly where burgers usually turn into hockey pucks. The air fryer's fast cycle keeps them juicy at temp.

🍔

Air fryer settings for Hamburgers

Fresh / thawed 375°F (191°C) 12 min Shake at 6 min

Conventional oven (for comparison) 425°F (218°C) 18 min —

🌡️ Safe internal temperature: 160°F / 71°C

160°F for all ground beef — no exceptions. Grinding distributes surface bacteria throughout the patty, so the whole thing must reach temp.

How to make it

Prep: Press a shallow dimple into the center of each raw patty. It stops the burger from doming into a meatball as the proteins contract.

  1. Divide beef into two loose patties, slightly wider than your buns.
  2. Dimple the center of each and season both sides.
  3. Place in the basket with space between them.
  4. Cook at 375°F for 6 minutes, flip, then 6 minutes to 160°F.
  5. Add cheese for the last minute, then rest 2 minutes before assembling.

Ingredients

  • 2/3 lb ground beef (80/20)
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 slices cheese (optional)
  • 2 burger buns

Pro tips

  • ✅80/20 ground beef is the right ratio. Anything leaner dries out before it reaches 160°F.
  • ✅Add cheese in the final 60 seconds with the basket closed — it melts perfectly in the residual heat.
  • ✅Don't press the patty while cooking. You're squeezing out the fat that makes it good.

Shake and doneness details

Flip at 6 minutes with a thin spatula. The USDA safe minimum for this food is 160°F (71°C), and air frying does not change that requirement.

Common questions

Frozen patties without thawing?

Yes. 375°F for about 16 minutes, flipping at 8. Season after the surface thaws at around 4 minutes so the seasoning sticks.

Do burgers make a mess in the air fryer?

Some fat drips. Add a tablespoon of water to the drawer below the basket to prevent smoking, and clean the basket while it's still warm.
