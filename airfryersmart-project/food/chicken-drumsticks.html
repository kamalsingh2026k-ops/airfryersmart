🍗 Air Fryer Chicken Drumsticks: Temperature, Time & Tips

Cheap, kid-friendly, and they cook more evenly than you'd expect because the meat wraps a single straight bone.

🍗

Air fryer settings for Chicken Drumsticks

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 390°F (199°C) 22 min Shake at 12 min
From frozen 360°F (182°C) 32 min Shake once halfway
Conventional oven (for comparison) 425°F (218°C) 40 min —

🌡️ Safe internal temperature: 165°F / 74°C

165°F minimum measured against — not touching — the bone. Dark meat is more pleasant at 175°F.

How to make it

Prep: Dry the skin well and season aggressively; drumsticks have a high skin-to-meat ratio so surface seasoning carries the whole dish.

  1. Pat drumsticks dry and rub with oil.
  2. Combine dry seasonings and coat evenly.
  3. Arrange in a single layer with space between.
  4. Cook at 390°F for 12 minutes, turn, then 10 minutes more.
  5. Check for 165°F minimum and rest 3 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 6 chicken drumsticks
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne (optional)

Pro tips

  • ✅Stand them upright leaning against the basket wall if you're cooking six — more airflow, same footprint.
  • ✅A 10-minute dry brine with salt draws moisture out then reabsorbs it, seasoning the meat deeper.
  • ✅Brush with BBQ sauce only in the last 3 minutes to prevent burning.

Shake and doneness details

Turn each drumstick a half-rotation at 12 minutes. The USDA safe minimum for this food is 165°F (74°C), and air frying does not change that requirement.

Common questions

Can I stack drumsticks?

Only if you shake and rearrange every 8 minutes, and add about 5 minutes total. A single layer is meaningfully crispier.

Do drumsticks need oil?

A light coat helps seasoning adhere and speeds browning, but the skin has enough fat to crisp on its own if you're avoiding added oil.
