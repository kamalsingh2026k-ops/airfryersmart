🥔 Air Fryer Baby Potatoes: Temperature, Time & Tips

Crackly skin, creamy interior. Air-fried baby potatoes are one of the few side dishes that genuinely improve on the oven version.

🥔

Air fryer settings for Baby Potatoes

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 390°F (199°C) 20 min Shake at 10 min
From frozen 400°F (204°C) 22 min Shake once halfway
Conventional oven (for comparison) 425°F (218°C) 35 min —

Done when a knife or skewer slides through with no resistance and the skin is blistered.

How to make it

Prep: Halve anything bigger than a golf ball. A 5-minute par-boil before air frying gives you the fluffiest possible interior.

  1. Halve potatoes and optionally par-boil 5 minutes, then drain and dry.
  2. Toss with oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder and rosemary.
  3. Arrange cut-side down in a single layer.
  4. Cook at 390°F for 10 minutes.
  5. Shake and cook 10 more minutes until crisp and fork-tender.
  6. Toss with parsley before serving.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Pro tips

  • ✅Par-boiling for 5 minutes then roughing them up in the colander creates edges that fry into crunchy shards.
  • ✅Halve them cut-side down for a caramelized flat face.
  • ✅Add fresh herbs like rosemary at the end, not the start — they burn at 390°F.

Common questions

Whole baked potato in the air fryer?

Yes. A large russet takes about 40 minutes at 390°F. Pierce it several times, rub with oil and salt, and turn once at 20 minutes.

Do I need to par-boil?

No, but it produces a noticeably fluffier interior and crispier crust. Skip it on a weeknight, do it for guests.
