🥩 Air Fryer Pork Tenderloin: Temperature, Time & Tips

An entire dinner protein in 20 minutes. Tenderloin is uniform in shape, which is exactly what an air fryer handles best.

🥩

Conventional oven (for comparison) 425°F (218°C) 28 min —

🌡️ Safe internal temperature: 145°F / 63°C

145°F with a 3-minute rest. Tenderloin is extremely lean — past 150°F it turns dry and grainy fast.

How to make it

Prep: Remove the silverskin, the shiny membrane along one side. It doesn't break down and will make the meat curl as it tightens.

  1. Trim silverskin and pat dry.
  2. Rub with oil and mustard, then coat in the seasoning blend.
  3. Place diagonally in the basket, halving it if needed.
  4. Cook at 400°F for 10 minutes.
  5. Rotate and cook 10 more minutes to 145°F.
  6. Rest 5 minutes, then slice into 1/2-inch medallions.

Ingredients

  • 1 pork tenderloin (1-1.25 lb)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Pro tips

  • ✅Cut it in half if it doesn't fit — two shorter pieces cook the same as one long one.
  • ✅Silverskin removal takes 60 seconds and dramatically improves texture.
  • ✅Rest 5 minutes then slice into medallions across the grain.

Shake and doneness details

Rotate a quarter-turn at 10 minutes to brown all four sides. The USDA safe minimum for this food is 145°F (63°C), and air frying does not change that requirement.

Common questions

Tenderloin vs pork loin — same thing?

No, and it matters. Tenderloin is a small 1 lb muscle that cooks in 20 minutes. Pork loin is a wide 3-4 lb roast needing about 45 minutes at 350°F.

Should I sear it first?

Not necessary. At 400°F the air fryer browns the exterior on its own within the cook time.
