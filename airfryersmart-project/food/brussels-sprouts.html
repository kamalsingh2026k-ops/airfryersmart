🥬 Air Fryer Brussels Sprouts: Temperature, Time & Tips

The vegetable that converts skeptics. Air frying caramelizes the outer leaves into something closer to a chip than a boiled sprout.

🥬

Air fryer settings for Brussels Sprouts

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 380°F (193°C) 15 min Shake at 8 min
From frozen 380°F (193°C) 18 min Shake once halfway
Conventional oven (for comparison) 425°F (218°C) 25 min —

Done when a knife slides into the core with no resistance and the outer leaves are dark and crisp.

How to make it

Prep: Halve them through the stem and trim the dry base. Cut-side down for the first half of cooking gives you a caramelized flat face.

  1. Trim and halve sprouts through the stem.
  2. Toss with oil, salt, pepper and garlic.
  3. Arrange cut-side down in a single layer.
  4. Cook at 380°F for 8 minutes.
  5. Shake and cook 7 more minutes until browned and tender.
  6. Finish with balsamic if using.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 1.5 tbsp olive oil
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar (optional finish)

Pro tips

  • ✅Cut-side down first — that flat surface against hot metal is where the deep browning happens.
  • ✅Don't rescue the loose leaves that fall off. They turn into crisps and everyone fights over them.
  • ✅A drizzle of balsamic or honey in the last 2 minutes glazes without burning.

Shake and doneness details

Shake hard at 8 minutes. The loose outer leaves that fall off become the best part.

Common questions

Frozen Brussels sprouts?

They work but won't get as crisp because of ice content. 380°F for 18 minutes, shaking twice, and don't thaw first.

Why are mine bitter?

Undercooking. Bitterness breaks down with caramelization — push them 3-4 minutes further than feels right.
