The conversion rules

RuleAdjustment
Base ruleOven temp − 25°F (15°C) · oven time × 0.75–0.8
Baked goods & dessertsTemp − 40°F · time × 0.8
Frozen food+35% time (5–15 min extra) · never thaw breaded items
Large batch (~2 lb)+13% time — crowding blocks airflow
Family batch (3 lb+)+25% time — or cook in two batches
Oven-style / toaster-oven models+10°F and +10% time vs basket models
Reheating leftovers3–4 min at 350°F — heat to 165°F internal

Fix-it table

ProblemFix
Soggy, not crispySingle layer · preheat 3 min · shake halfway
Burning on topLower temp 10–15°F · move food away from element
Smoke during cooking2 tbsp water in the drawer · clean the element weekly
Food stickingPerforated parchment · oil the basket · never aerosol spray
Uneven browningShake more often · don't crowd · check the fan vent is clean
