        Frequently asked questions

        What is the rule for converting oven recipes to an air fryer?
        Reduce the oven temperature by 25°F (about 15°C) and cut the cooking time by roughly 20–25%. A recipe calling for 400°F for 30 minutes becomes about 375°F for 23 minutes. This works because an air fryer is a small convection oven: the fan drives hot air directly across the food surface, so heat transfers much faster than in a large static oven. Read the full conversion guide →
        Do I need to preheat an air fryer?
        For most foods a 3-minute preheat gives noticeably better browning, especially steak, chicken skin and frozen fries. You can skip it for long cooks over 25 minutes and for reheating, where those few minutes make little difference to the finished result.

        Why do different air fryer brands need different settings?
        Basket-style air fryers such as Ninja and Philips have a small chamber with the fan directly above the food, so they run effectively hotter than the dial suggests. Oven-style and toaster-oven air fryers have a much larger cavity, weaker airflow and more thermal mass, so they typically need around 10°F more and about 10% longer. That is exactly what the brand selector above adjusts for.
        How much longer does frozen food take?
        Add roughly 35% more time, usually 5–15 extra minutes depending on the length of the cook. Never thaw breaded or par-fried frozen foods first — thawing releases water into the coating and you lose the crispiness that made you reach for the air fryer in the first place.
        Does a fuller basket change the cooking time?
        Yes. Crowding blocks the airflow that does the actual cooking. Add about 13% more time for a large batch and 25% for a family-size load, shake more often, and accept that two smaller batches will always beat one overloaded basket for crispiness.
        What internal temperature should food reach?
        Air frying does not change food safety rules. USDA minimums still apply: 165°F for all poultry, 160°F for ground meats, 145°F with a 3-minute rest for whole cuts of beef, pork and lamb, and 145°F for fish. See the full safe temperature table above.
        Can I convert baking recipes?
        Yes, but drop the temperature further — around 40°F below the oven temperature, which is what the “Baked Goods” category does automatically. The fan is aggressive, and at normal baking temperatures the tops of cakes and cookies brown long before the centres set. Never put empty parchment paper into a preheating air fryer, as it can lift into the heating element.
