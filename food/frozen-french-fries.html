🍟 Air Fryer Frozen French Fries: Temperature, Time & Tips

The reason most people buy an air fryer. Frozen fries come out closer to fast-food quality than any oven can manage.

🍟

Air fryer settings for Frozen French Fries

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 400°F (204°C) 15 min Shake at 6 & 11 min
From frozen 400°F (204°C) 16 min Shake once halfway
Conventional oven (for comparison) 450°F (232°C) 25 min —

No internal temp needed. Fries are done when the outside is golden and rigid and the inside is fluffy.

How to make it

Prep: Straight from the freezer. Never thaw fries — thawed potato goes limp and soggy.

  1. Pour fries into the basket straight from the freezer.
  2. Cook at 400°F for 6 minutes.
  3. Shake vigorously and cook 5 more minutes.
  4. Shake again and cook a final 5 minutes until deep golden.
  5. Salt immediately while hot so it sticks.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb frozen French fries
  • Optional: 1 tsp oil spray
  • Salt to taste

Pro tips

  • ✅Two shakes minimum. Fries clump and the clumped faces stay pale.
  • ✅Cook no more than a 1.5-inch deep layer. Beyond that, the top browns and the bottom steams.
  • ✅No oil needed for most frozen fries; they're par-fried already. A light spray helps thin shoestring cuts.

Shake and doneness details

Shake twice — at 6 and 11 minutes. Fries stick to each other more than any other food.

Common questions

Do I need to preheat for frozen fries?

A 3-minute preheat gives measurably crispier results because the fries hit hot metal immediately instead of warming up slowly with the chamber.

Why are my fries soggy?

Overcrowding, almost always. Halve the batch. Also make sure you never thaw them first.
