🍕 Air Fryer Reheating Leftover Pizza: Temperature, Time & Tips

The single best use of an air fryer that nobody mentions in the manual. Three minutes restores a crisp base a microwave can never produce.

🍕

Air fryer settings for Reheating Leftover Pizza

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 350°F (177°C) 4 min No shake needed
From frozen 350°F (177°C) 6 min Small pieces, watch closely
Conventional oven (for comparison) 375°F (191°C) 10 min —

🌡️ Safe internal temperature: 165°F / 74°C

Reheat leftovers to 165°F throughout, per USDA guidance for previously cooked food.

How to make it

Prep: None. Straight from the fridge into the basket.

  1. Place slices flat in the basket without overlapping.
  2. Cook at 350°F for 3 minutes.
  3. Check the cheese; add 1 more minute if needed.
  4. Serve immediately.

Ingredients

  • 2-3 leftover pizza slices

Pro tips

  • ✅350°F, not higher. Hot air hits the cheese first and 400°F browns the toppings before the base warms.
  • ✅Lay slices flat, not overlapping. Overlap creates a soggy seam.
  • ✅A tiny sprinkle of water on the crust before reheating steams it soft, then the air crisps it — bakery trick.

Shake and doneness details

No flip needed. The USDA safe minimum for this food is 165°F (74°C), and air frying does not change that requirement.

Common questions

Can I stack slices?

No. Stacking traps steam and you get a soft, floppy crust — exactly what you were trying to avoid.

Frozen leftover pizza?

350°F for about 6 minutes straight from frozen. There's no need to thaw.
