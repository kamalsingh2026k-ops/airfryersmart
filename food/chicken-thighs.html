🍗 Air Fryer Chicken Thighs: Temperature, Time & Tips

Forgiving, fatty, and nearly impossible to ruin. Bone-in skin-on thighs are the best value cut for air frying.

🍗

Air fryer settings for Chicken Thighs

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 380°F (193°C) 25 min Shake at 15 min
From frozen 360°F (182°C) 33 min Shake once halfway
Conventional oven (for comparison) 425°F (218°C) 40 min —

🌡️ Safe internal temperature: 165°F / 74°C

165°F is the safe minimum, but thighs are better at 175-180°F — the extra time melts connective tissue without drying the meat.

How to make it

Prep: Trim excess flaps of skin and fat, which can smoke. Season under the skin as well as on top.

  1. Pat thighs dry and trim excess fat.
  2. Rub oil and spices over and under the skin.
  3. Place skin-side down in the basket.
  4. Cook at 380°F for 15 minutes.
  5. Flip skin-side up and cook 10 more minutes until skin is crackling and internal temp reads 175°F.

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in skin-on chicken thighs
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Pro tips

  • ✅Thighs render a lot of fat — add a tablespoon of water to the drawer beneath the basket to stop smoke.
  • ✅Unlike breast, thighs benefit from going past 165°F. Aim for 175°F for silky texture.
  • ✅Skin-side down first, then flip: the rendered fat drains and the skin crisps in its own fat.

Shake and doneness details

Start skin-side down, flip skin-side up at 15 minutes for the final crisping stretch. The USDA safe minimum for this food is 165°F (74°C), and air frying does not change that requirement.

Common questions

Boneless vs bone-in timing?

Boneless skinless thighs need about 18 minutes at 380°F. Bone-in takes 25 because the bone slows heat transfer to the center.

Why is my air fryer smoking with thighs?

Rendered chicken fat hitting the hot heating element. Pour a tablespoon or two of water into the outer drawer, or line beneath the basket with a small piece of foil that doesn't block airflow.
