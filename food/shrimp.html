🍤 Air Fryer Shrimp: Temperature, Time & Tips

Six minutes, start to finish. Shrimp is the fastest protein in the air fryer and the easiest to ruin by walking away.

🍤

🌡️ Safe internal temperature: 145°F / 63°C

145°F, but visual cues are more practical: shrimp are done when opaque and curled into a loose C. A tight O means overcooked.

How to make it

Prep: Thaw and dry thoroughly. Wet shrimp release water and poach themselves instead of roasting.

  1. Thaw shrimp fully and pat very dry.
  2. Toss with oil, garlic, paprika, salt and pepper flakes.
  3. Spread in a single layer in the basket.
  4. Cook at 400°F for 3 minutes, shake, then 3 minutes more.
  5. Pull the moment they're opaque and C-shaped; finish with lemon juice.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 lemon

Pro tips

  • ✅A loose C shape is perfect. A tight O is rubbery. This is the most useful doneness cue in cooking.
  • ✅Set a timer — the window between perfect and ruined is under 90 seconds.
  • ✅Larger shrimp are more forgiving. Jumbo 16-20 count gives you more margin for error.

Shake and doneness details

Shake at 3 minutes. With a 6-minute cook, that single shake matters a lot. The USDA safe minimum for this food is 145°F (63°C), and air frying does not change that requirement.

Common questions

Can I cook shrimp from frozen?

Yes — 400°F for about 9 minutes. Rinse off ice glaze and dry them first, or they'll steam. Shake twice during cooking.

Breaded coconut shrimp timing?

375°F for 8 minutes, flipping at 4. Spray with oil so the coconut toasts golden instead of drying out white.
