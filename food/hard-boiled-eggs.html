🥚 Air Fryer Hard "Boiled" Eggs: Temperature, Time & Tips

No water, no pot, no watching for a boil. And they peel more easily than pot-boiled eggs, which is the real selling point.

🥚

Air fryer settings for Hard "Boiled" Eggs

StateTemperatureTimeDuring cooking
Fresh / thawed 270°F (132°C) 15 min No shake needed
Conventional oven (for comparison) 325°F (163°C) 30 min —

15 minutes at 270°F gives a fully set yolk. 11 minutes gives a jammy center; 13 gives a soft-set yolk.

How to make it

Prep: Nothing. Place eggs straight from the fridge into the basket.

  1. Place eggs directly in the basket in a single layer, not touching.
  2. Cook at 270°F for 15 minutes.
  3. Transfer immediately to an ice water bath for 5 minutes.
  4. Peel under running water for the cleanest release.

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • Ice water for the bath

Pro tips

  • ✅Ice bath immediately after cooking. It stops the cooking and makes the shell release cleanly.
  • ✅Every air fryer differs by a minute or two — test one egg first and note your model's timing.
  • ✅Brown spots on the shell are harmless contact marks from the basket, not burns.

Shake and doneness details

No shake. Eggs crack if they roll into each other.

Common questions

Do the eggs explode?

No, as long as you stay at 270°F or below and use a proper ice bath afterward. Higher temperatures can crack shells.

How do I get soft-boiled?

11 minutes at 270°F gives a runny-jammy yolk; 13 minutes gives a soft-set center. Ice bath immediately either way.
